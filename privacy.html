Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026  ·  druginducedmito.org

This Privacy Policy describes how the Drug-Induced Mitochondrial Dysfunction (DIMD) Initiative/FQAD (druginducedmito.org), founded by Johanna Ihli, BSN, handles information collected through this website and the FQAD Registry. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors and registry participants.

This site is for educational and advocacy purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Nothing on this site establishes a patient-provider relationship.

1. Information We Collect

This website does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to access educational content.

Through the FQAD Registry intake form, we collect only the information you voluntarily provide, which may include:

  • Your role (patient, caregiver, clinician, researcher, or other)
  • The medication(s) of concern
  • Approximate symptom onset and duration
  • Body systems affected (selected from a checklist)
  • Optional free-text notes describing your experience
  • An optional email address, if you choose to provide one for follow-up

Because this information describes health experiences, we treat all registry submissions as sensitive health information, even when no name is attached.

No identifying information is required. Please do not include your name, date of birth, address, Social Security number, insurance information, or any other personal identifiers in your registry responses, including in free-text notes.

Technical information. Our website analytics, hosting provider, and registry form provider may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, approximate location, and time of access. This is used for site statistics, security, and spam prevention. We do not use it to identify registry participants, and it is not linked to your registry responses. See Sections 4 and 7 for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Registry submissions are used solely for the following purposes:

  • Research — building an evidence base for drug-induced mitochondrial dysfunction and related delayed multisystem injury patterns
  • Education — informing public and clinician awareness about DIMD and FQAD
  • Advocacy — supporting pharmacovigilance modernization, regulatory engagement, and policy development
  • Public awareness — communicating the scale and pattern of drug-induced injury to the public and policymakers

Registry information used in any publication, report, presentation, or advocacy document will be de-identified and presented in aggregate form. Before publication or external disclosure, findings will be reviewed to minimize the risk that individuals could be identified, including through unusual combinations of characteristics, small groups, or free-text descriptions. Identifiable submissions will not be published without your separate, explicit permission.

The FQAD Registry is a voluntary collection of patient-reported information. It is not currently a formal research study approved by an ethics board, and submitting to the registry does not enroll you in any study. Any future research use of registry information will follow the ethics review and consent requirements that apply to that specific use.

3. Email Address (Optional)

If you choose to provide an email address, it will be used only to:

  • Confirm receipt of your submission
  • Ask follow-up questions about your submission (infrequent)
  • Respond to your requests to access, correct, or withdraw your submission
  • Send occasional registry updates, only if you ask to receive them; you can stop these at any time by replying to any update or emailing us

Your email address will never be sold, shared, or used for commercial purposes. It will not be added to any marketing list.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Can See It

We do not sell, rent, or share your registry data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Registry submissions are collected and stored by Jotform (jotform.com), a third-party form service whose terms permit forms that collect health information, and a copy of each submission is delivered to our registry inbox (dimd-admin@proton.me), hosted by Proton Mail. Within the DIMD Initiative/FQAD, only Johanna Ihli, BSN, has access to individual submissions. Our service providers store this data on our behalf, and their authorized personnel may access it only as described in their own privacy policies.

Registry data may be shared only in de-identified, aggregate form with:

  • Researchers collaborating with the DIMD Initiative/FQAD on studies of drug-induced injury
  • Regulatory bodies or policymakers as part of advocacy efforts
  • Academic or public health publications

Individual submissions will not be shared with anyone outside the DIMD Initiative/FQAD without your explicit consent.

Third-party privacy policies:

  • Jotform (registry form): jotform.com/privacy
  • Proton Mail (registry inbox): proton.me/legal/privacy
  • GitHub (site hosting): GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Analytics (site statistics): policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

Registry submissions are kept for as long as the registry is active, to support long-term tracking of delayed and persistent effects. When submissions are no longer needed, they will be deleted from our Jotform account and from our registry inbox. We review stored submissions at least once a year and delete email addresses and other contact details that are no longer needed. Aggregate, de-identified findings that have already been published may be kept indefinitely.

6. Your Rights — Access, Correction, and Deletion

If you provided an email address or another way to identify your submission, you may ask at any time to see what you submitted, correct it, or withdraw and delete it by contacting us at dimd-admin@proton.me. We will respond as promptly as we can.

Deletion requests will be completed across the systems we control (our Jotform account and registry inbox), subject to applicable legal and technical limits, and we will confirm what was deleted. Copies held in service providers' backups are removed on their own schedules. Information already included in published aggregate findings cannot be removed from those publications, but your individual entry will be deleted.

If you submitted without an email address, we may not be able to locate your submission. If you contact us with details such as the date and time you submitted and what you entered, we will make a reasonable effort to find and delete it.

7. Cookies, Analytics, and Tracking

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, approximate location, and device type. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which collects aggregate page-view statistics without using cookies.

We use these tools only to understand overall site traffic. We do not use advertising tools or tracking pixels, we do not sell analytics data, and registry answers, email addresses, and other form values are not sent to analytics. You can block analytics cookies through your browser settings or with Google's opt-out tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

8. Website Security

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served over HTTPS, so information you enter is encrypted on its way to us. The registry form is served by Jotform over HTTPS. Our registry inbox is hosted by Proton Mail, which stores messages with encryption. Access to the registry form account and inbox is limited to Johanna Ihli, BSN.

We take reasonable measures to protect registry data, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Please do not include sensitive personal identifiers in your registry responses.

9. Children

This website is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted registry information, please contact us at dimd-admin@proton.me and we will remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. If we make a material change to how registry data is used or shared, we will not apply that change to past submissions without the consent of participants we can reach by email.

11. Contact

Johanna Ihli, BSN, is responsible for deciding how registry information is collected, used, and protected. For any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your registry data, please contact:
